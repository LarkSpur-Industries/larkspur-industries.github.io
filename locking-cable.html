WHY LOCKING CABLES?

Vibration Immune: Standard USB-C cables rely on friction. In FRC robots, heavy vibrations and impacts can wiggle connectors loose, causing momentary power loss or data disconnection. Our screw-locking mechanism physically secures the plug to the port.

High Power Handling: Rated for up to 240W, this cable easily handles the full 10A output of the Marigold-5 without voltage drop or overheating.

Industrial Durability: Reinforced strain relief and a tough outer jacket protect against abrasion and sharp edges inside your robot chassis. The locking screws are knurled for easy hand-tightening.
