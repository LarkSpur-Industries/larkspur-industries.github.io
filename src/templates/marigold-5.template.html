WHY MARIGOLD-5?

Brownout-Proof Power: FRC batteries sag from 12V down to 7V during matches. Most common adapters cut out around 10V, rebooting your co-processor exactly when you need it most. Marigold-5 operates reliably down to 6V, keeping connected devices stable.

Built for Hungry Devices: Vision systems require significant processing power. With Marigold-5, you can power a single co-processor or add another device using the auxiliary port. The 10A total output means you don't have to choose between devices.

Quick Replacement: The push button input connector and USB-C locking connection means you can swap a failed unit in minutes. Perfect for when you're between qualification matches. No soldering, no DIY wiring, just a few minutes and you're back in the game.
