The Marigold-5 locking cable uses retention screws to keep its USB-C connections secure during robot vibration and impacts.

## Key information

- Locking USB-C connectors on both ends
- High-power-rated conductors
- Flexible cable with a durable TPE jacket
- Secure each retention screw after fully inserting the USB-C connector.
- Route the cable with strain relief and away from sharp edges or moving mechanisms.
- Inspect the connector and screw before competition matches.
- Do not overtighten the retention screw.
