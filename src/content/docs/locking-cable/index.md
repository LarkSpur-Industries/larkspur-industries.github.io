The Marigold-5 locking cable is a specialized 12-inch USB-C power cable with retention screws on both ends. Its 20 AWG power conductors are designed to reduce voltage drop at high loads.

:::note[Purchase availability]
The locking cable costs **$7.99 USD** and is available only with the purchase of a Marigold-5 for now.
:::

## Installation

1. Fully insert each USB-C connector before securing its retention screw.
2. Tighten each retention screw until secure. Do not overtighten.
3. Perform a tug test, the cable should not pull out.
4. Route the cable with strain relief and keep it away from sharp edges and moving mechanisms.
5. Inspect the connectors, jacket, and screws before powering.

Results use a 5.1V source from a Marigold-5. Our cable measured **4.972V at 3A**, **4.826V at 6A**, and **4.668V at 9A**. These results equal voltage drops of 0.128V, 0.274V, and 0.432V.
