The Marigold-5 locking cable uses a retention screw to keep its USB-C connection secure during robot vibration and impacts. It is intended for the locking USB-C receptacle on Marigold-5.

## Key information

- Secure the retention screw after fully inserting the USB-C connector.
- Route the cable with strain relief and away from sharp edges or moving mechanisms.
- Inspect the connector and screw before competition matches.
- Do not overtighten the retention screw.

Additional dimensional and compatibility information will be added as it becomes available.
