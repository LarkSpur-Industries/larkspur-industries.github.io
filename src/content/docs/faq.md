## When do preorders open?

Offseason preorders open Saturday, September 26, 2026. The first production batch is limited to 120 units and is expected to ship in mid to late October.

## Why is Marigold-5 sold in batches?

Larkspur Industries is a small hardware company. Building in controlled batches helps us manage component supply, assembly, and quality checks while keeping pricing fair.

## How often will Marigold-5 be restocked?

There is no fixed restock schedule yet. Future batches will be planned around demand and component availability, with dates announced on the website and the CD thread when they are confirmed.

## How is Marigold-5 different from a typical automotive 12V-to-5V converter?

Many automotive and general-purpose converters require an 8-9V minimum input. An FRC battery can sag to 6-7V during a demanding match, which may cause those converters to lose regulation and reboot a co-processor. Marigold-5 is designed to maintain its regulated 5.1V output down to a 6V input and provides mounting and wiring intended for competition robots.

## What happens if the first batch sells out?

Preorders will close when all 120 units are reserved. We will then announce the next batch when its production and shipping schedule is confirmed.
